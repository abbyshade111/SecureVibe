# ADR-074: Why a caught prompt injection was stopped, written down with it (C12.1.2, in part)

**Status:** accepted, 9 October 2026, in the pull request that builds it; proposed with the backlog claim (0085, part
21). Asked for by the owner ("please continue when ready"). Written by session paper-facts.

**Governs:** `crates/sv-check/src/ai.rs`, where the check this record adds, probe.ai-safety-decision-detailed, reads the
line probe.ai-injection-logged finds (ADR-071 and ADR-073 govern the same file). A pull request that changes what it
says about this changes this record too, or says in its description why it does not (`docs/adr/README.md`).

## Context

AISVS C12.1.2 (level 2) reads: "Verify that safety filtering and policy decisions are logged with sufficient detail to
support audit, debugging, and forensic analysis of content moderation systems." Nothing in StackVet speaks to it. After
the run, the AI checks look in the app's output for a line recording the textbook prompt injection they sent as caught
(C12.2.1, `probe.ai-injection-logged`). A bare "blocked" line says that something was stopped, not why or when, which is
what an audit or an investigation asks first.

## Decision

- **The same line, read again:** the line that recorded the injection as caught.
- **Credited, marked in part (ADR-053),** when it carries both a reason (a `reason`, `category`, `rule`, `policy`,
  `score` or `label` field, in JSON or `key=value`) and a time (a timestamp, as the log checks read one). The credit says
  whether it also names the signed-in user or a session. One kind of safety decision, the injection screen, is seen.
- **Never a finding:** the detail may be recorded in another system. A line without a reason or a time is said, naming
  what was not seen.
- **Not asked** when no line recorded the injection as caught, which C12.2.1 already says.

## Alternatives considered

- **Every part (reason, time, user) required for credit.** Rejected: an injection sent without signing in has no user
  to record, and the requirement's "sufficient detail" is met by what the decision was and when.
- **The kill-switch log (C12.4.3) at the same time.** Not here: nothing reads the second copy's output yet.

## Where the build differs from the proposal

- **Said, not left unasked, when no line recorded the injection as caught,** and when the app wrote nothing to its
  output, so C12.1.2 is spoken to whenever the injection was sent.
- **The line read is the first that recorded the injection as caught,** the same test C12.2.1 uses.
- **The time is read by the log checks' own timestamp reader,** which now has a small public wrapper,
  `logs::has_timestamp`.
