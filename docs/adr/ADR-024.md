# ADR-024: An unanswered data list holds the app to ASVS level 2

**Status:** accepted. Decided on 27 September 2026 at the owner's word ("let's fix the personal data starter file
issue"), built the same day (commit `b02efec`, merged through #265), and written down here on 4 October 2026, because
the first weekly review of the decision records found it had no record (BACKLOG, "Records owed", item 4).

## Context

OWASP ASVS has three levels, and the level an app is held to decides how many requirements apply. `sv` chooses it
from two answers in `securevibe.toml`: who the app is for (`[app] audience`), and what information it holds about
people (`[data] categories`). An app for customers or the public is held to level 2. So is one that holds anything on
the sensitive list (financial, payment-card, health, government-id, and children). An app for its owner alone or a
team, holding nothing sensitive, is held to level 1.

ADR-015 says that silence is not a "no": a question the owner has not answered leaves the requirements that turn on
it not assessed, never not applicable. It speaks of the yes-or-no facts that decide which requirements apply, and says
nothing of the level.

The starter file `sv init` wrote once held `categories = []`, which reads as "the app holds nothing about people". An
owner who never looked at that line got level 1, on a "no" they had never said.

## Decision

A data list nobody filled in counts as holding something sensitive, so the app is held to level 2. In the words
above `Manifest::target_level` (`crates/sv-manifest/src/lib.rs`): "level 1 is a claim that nothing sensitive is
held, and a list nobody filled in makes no claim."

- No `[data]` section, an empty one, or the line still commented out is unanswered: level 2.
- `categories = []` is an answer, "nothing about people", and with an audience of just the owner or a team gives
  level 1.
- The starter file now writes the line commented out (`# categories = ?`), with a comment saying what to put there.
- Only an app for its owner or a team can move: one for customers or the public is at level 2 by its audience,
  whatever the list says.

This carries ADR-015 into choosing the level, in the direction ADR-015 already takes: when unsure, more requirements
rather than fewer. It differs in one way. Under ADR-015 an unanswered question leaves its requirements not assessed;
here there is no requirement to leave unassessed, because the level decides which requirements apply at all, so the
unanswered list resolves to the stricter of the two answers.

## Alternatives considered

- **Level 1 when the list is unanswered.** What `sv` did before. Set aside for the reason in the Context: it turns
  silence into the answer that asks least of the app. Nothing else was written down as considered.
- **Not assessed.** Not workable for the level itself: every requirement's applicability depends on it, so "not
  assessed" would leave the whole report unassessed. Not recorded as considered at the time; said here as the reason
  the ADR-015 form could not be copied over unchanged.

## Consequences

- An owner who has not answered is held to more requirements than one who answers that the app holds nothing about
  people, which is the intent.
- The report says why. When the unanswered list is the only reason for level 2, `sv scope` prints a note and the report
  lists a gap, "What information the app holds about people", saying the level becomes 1 if nothing listed is
  sensitive (`Manifest::level_from_unanswered_data`). For an app for customers or the public the note is not shown,
  since the list is not what holds it at level 2.
- The owner lowers the level by answering, never by `sv` guessing: `sv` does not edit the manifest or change the level
  on the owner's behalf.
- `target_level` never returns 3. Level 3 requirements are counted only as "above this level".

## How it is held

`data_nobody_described_is_not_a_quiet_no` (unanswered, an empty `[data]`, and `[]`; the note only for an unanswered
list, and not for a public app), `sensitive_data_raises_the_target_level`, and
`the_starter_file_leaves_the_data_unanswered`, in `crates/sv-manifest/src/lib.rs`; and
`an_unanswered_data_list_is_held_to_level_2_and_says_why` in `crates/sv-cli/tests/data_unanswered.rs`, which runs
`sv report` both ways.
