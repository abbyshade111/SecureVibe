The project's security faults against the OWASP Top 10:2025

Four kinds of evidence, kept apart: weaknesses in SecureVibe's own code; places where a check reported "clean" when it had not looked; what v1 flagged when it checked itself; and what SecureVibe found in the apps it checked. Each item is placed through a CWE on the category's official 2025 list. The full account, with a source for every item, is TOP10.md.
