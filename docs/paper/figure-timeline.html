How the project unfolded, 16 September to 4 October 2026

Two versions over nineteen days: v1, which builds an app from a fixed template and checks it, and sv, which checks an app written in any language by anyone. On 26 September sv replaced v1 at the top of the repository. From 29 September a comparison study outside the repository used sv on five AI-built apps, and on 4 October reviewed its code in depth. The detail is in TIMELINE.md.
