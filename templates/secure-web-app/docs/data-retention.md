# How long data is kept, and how it is removed

No automatic retention period is configured yet. Data is kept until a person deletes their own account or an administrator deletes it.

## What a person can do themselves

- **Download a copy of their data** — Your account → Download your data — exports everything the app has recorded for their account as a single file.
- **Delete their account** — Your account → Delete account (asks them to confirm their password first). This removes their sign-in details and, for each kind of record listed below, either deletes or removes the personal parts of their records.

## Records affected by deletion

No entities have registered a deletion handler yet — add one with `registerEntity({ deleteForUser })` as new features are added.

## Automatic retention

To turn this on, set `RETENTION_MONTHS` in `.env` to the number of months you want to keep records for.

## The security event log

Kept for at least 400 days (`AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS`) so an incident can still be investigated after records are deleted; it never stores the personal data itself, only that an action happened.

Every deletion is recorded as a `data.deleted` security event before the data disappears (see docs/logging.md).
